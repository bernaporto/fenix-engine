How to play

Select your piece, then an empty highlighted neighbor. Move one tile horizontally or vertically. Moving ends your turn.

Click or tap the 3D board or use the tile buttons. On either board, use arrow keys to navigate, Enter or Space to select, and Escape to clear selection.

Sandbox rules: no captures, combat, or win condition yet. Pass if you cannot move.
